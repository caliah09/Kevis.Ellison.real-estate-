Privacy

Last updated August 2026

This website does not collect personal information, set cookies, or track visitors. There are no analytics, no advertising pixels, and no contact forms. If you want to reach Kevis Ellison, you call or email him directly, and he receives only what you choose to send.

What the site collects

Nothing. Every page here is a static file. There is no account to create, no form to fill in, and no script that records what you look at or how long you stay. Photographs, fonts, and property details are served from this site itself rather than pulled from an outside service, so browsing these pages does not report your visit to any third party.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's servers record technical information such as IP addresses in order to deliver pages and protect against abuse. That logging is GitHub's, not Kevis Ellison's, and it is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Kevis Ellison does not have access to those logs.

When you get in touch

Calling or emailing gives Kevis Ellison whatever you share — typically your name, phone number or email address, and what you are looking for. That information is used to respond to you and to help with your real estate transaction. It is not sold, rented, or traded, and it is not shared except where it is necessary to carry out the work you have asked for (for example, with the brokerage, a lender, a title company, or where the law requires it).

Ask him to delete your details at any time and he will.

Links to other sites

Property cards on this site link out to listing pages on realtor.com and valleymls.com. Those are separate companies with their own privacy practices, and this policy does not cover them. Once you follow one of those links you are subject to whatever that site does.

Children

This site is meant for adults conducting real estate business and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If the site ever starts collecting anything — a contact form, for instance, or analytics — this page will be updated to say so before that goes live, and the date at the top will change.
