# Social media captions

How Kevis's Sold and Under Contract posts are written. Use this for every new caption so they all sound like him.

## Rules

- **Sound like Kevis talking.** Write in the first person, warm and upbeat, in short paragraphs.
- **No long dashes.** Use a period or comma instead.
- **Open with "Another one"** and the status, plus one emoji:
  - Sold: `Another one SOLD! 🏡`
  - Under contract: `Another one under contract! 🎉`
- **Address and city next,** then the home's facts: beds, baths, square feet, and lot size if known. Use only facts from the MLS or Zillow. Never guess.
- **Market line:** homes are moving fast in Decatur or North Alabama.
- **Deals line:** say he works hard to get his clients a great deal. Never promise a price, a deal or a result.
- **Call to action:** message me or call **(256) 345-4901**.
- **Sign off exactly:** `I'm Kevis, a Realtor with Linda Blue Real Estate.` Never "Kevis with Kevis Ellison Real Estate."
- **Hashtags, always, in this order:**
  `#DecaturAL #DecaturRealEstate #NorthAlabamaRealEstate #NorthAlabamaHomes #ValleyMLS`
  For a home outside Decatur, add the town too, for example `#HarvestAL`.

## Before posting

- **Sold** posts go up only after the closing. Until then, call it **under contract**.
- **Someone else's listing** (for example 241 Shortleaf Ln, which KW Huntsville listed): don't imply Kevis listed it. Say what his role was, such as the buyer's agent, once it's confirmed, and name the listing brokerage.
- **Remove anything you can't confirm,** such as "a smooth closing" or "it went fast."
