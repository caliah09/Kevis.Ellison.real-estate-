# Social media captions

How Kevis's posts are written, based on captions he actually posted. Use this for every new caption.

## The style

- **One short paragraph.** Three to five sentences, conversational, with the hashtags at the end of the same paragraph.
- **Open with a hook:** a question or a value angle.
  - `Ever wonder what $65 a square foot actually looks like?`
  - `Great space for the price, under $200,000.`
- **Facts in one sentence:** the address, what happened (just sold, just hit the market, under contract), the price, then beds, baths and square feet. Write out "square feet" and use "bed" and "bath": `3 bed, 3 bath, 2,153 square feet`.
- **One or two standout features,** only when confirmed: `Full brick`, `a huge fenced-in backyard`, a big lot.
- **A value or urgency line:** `That's the kind of value that moves fast.`
- **End with a simple call to action:**
  - Sold or under contract: `Want first look at the next one? Message me and I'll keep you posted before it hits the market.`
  - For sale: `Send me a message and I'll get you in to see it.`
- **No sign-off line, no emojis and no long dashes.** Use commas and periods.
- **Hashtags, always, in this order:**
  `#DecaturAL #DecaturRealEstate #NorthAlabamaRealEstate #NorthAlabamaHomes #ValleyMLS`
  For a home outside Decatur, add the town too, for example `#HarvestAL`.

### Price per square foot

Divide the price by the square feet and round to a whole dollar. Check the math before posting.

| Home | Math | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 211 13th Ave NW | $140,000 / 2,153 = $65.03 | $65 a square foot |
| 306 12th Ave NW | $95,000 / 1,600 = $59.38 | $59 a square foot |

## Before posting

- **Sold** posts go up only after the closing. Until then, call it **under contract**.
- **Someone else's listing** (for example 241 Shortleaf Ln, which KW Huntsville listed): don't imply Kevis listed it. Say what his role was once it's confirmed, and name the listing brokerage.
- **Use only facts from the MLS, Zillow or Kevis himself.** Never promise a price, a deal or a result.
